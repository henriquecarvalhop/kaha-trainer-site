Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what Stoic collects, why, and what you can do about it. Stoic ("the app") is published by CloudArbitration LTDA ("we", "us"), the same company behind Kaha Trainer and DietCoach. You can reach us at info@kahatrainer.com.

1. The short version

You can use Stoic without an account: everything then stays on your iPhone. If you create one, we store your journal, practice progress, favorites and preferences so they follow you to a new phone. Messages you send to the AI chat go to Anthropic (Claude) through our server only to write the reply; we do not store the conversation. Usage statistics sit under a random identifier that is not connected to your account. The free version shows ads, which may use an advertising identifier only if you allow it. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account information — optional

An account is optional. In guest mode we do not create an account and your data stays on the device. If you choose to sign in, you can use Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google or a one-time code sent to your email. We then store your email address, a display name and an account identifier. With Apple, we receive whatever Apple sends us — which may be a private relay address instead of your real email; we also keep the token Apple issues so we can revoke the sign-in when you delete your account. With Google, we receive your name and email from Google. There are no passwords.

2.2 Your practice and journal

Everything you create in the app, which is the reason it exists:

  • Journal entries: the template used, what you wrote and the emotions you picked, plus your own templates
  • The daily practice: morning and evening steps, your streak and its weekly protection
  • Lessons completed, the active path, challenges started and the days checked
  • Saved quotes
  • Your answers in the welcome screens (goals, experience, favorite philosopher) and your reminder times

This data is stored on your device. When you are signed in, it is also synced to our database so it is backed up and available on your other devices. In guest mode it is never sent to us. Reading progress in the library, appearance settings and AI chat conversations stay on the device only.

2.3 AI chat

The chat lets you talk with an AI that speaks in the style of Marcus Aurelius, Seneca or Epictetus, based on their writings. It is not the philosopher. It requires an account, and the app asks for your agreement before the first conversation. When you use it:

  • Your messages, the recent history of that conversation (up to the last 20 messages), the philosopher you chose, the quote or lesson you opened the chat from and your first name go through our server to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, which writes the reply.
  • The text of a journal entry is sent only when you ask for a comment on that entry. The weekly summary sends your entries from the last seven days, only when you tap the button to create it.
  • Your email, account identifier and the rest of your journal are not sent to Anthropic.
  • We do not store the conversation on our server. We record only how many messages you sent and what they cost, to apply your plan's limits. Conversations are kept on your device and deleted when you sign out or delete your account.
  • The first AI comment on a journal entry is saved with that entry, so it syncs with your journal.
  • Under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models. Anthropic may retain it for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring.

2.4 Subscriptions

Everything in Stoic is free. Two optional plans, No ads and No ads + AI, are sold as in-app subscriptions. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To unlock the plan you paid for, we store the subscription's product, status, renewal and expiry dates and Apple's transaction identifiers, linked to your account.

2.5 Usage analytics and crash reports

To understand which screens deserve to exist, the app records product events: which screen was opened, whether a lesson was completed or a journal entry saved, whether an ad was shown or skipped. Each event carries a random identifier generated on your device at install, a session number, the event name, the app version, the platform and the time. Events never include anything you wrote — journal text, intentions or chat messages.

That identifier is not linked to your account or your email. It is recreated if you delete and reinstall the app.

We also use Google Firebase: Firebase Analytics records app installs and ad impressions to measure our ad campaigns, and Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes (device model, iOS version, app version and the technical state at the moment of the crash) so we can fix it.

2.6 Advertising

The free version of Stoic is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads never appear on the Today screen, in the journal, in a lesson, while reading or in the chat. Depending on your choices and region, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), general device and network information, and ad interaction data.

On iOS, the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking when the system asks. If you decline, the app works exactly the same and you still see ads — just less relevant ones. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it later in the app. Subscribers to either plan see no ads. Google's handling of this data follows its own policies; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

2.7 Notifications

Practice reminders are scheduled locally on your device; their content does not pass through us. To send other notifications we use Firebase Cloud Messaging: if you allow notifications, we store the device's push token together with the same random install identifier, your account (if you are signed in), language, time zone, app version and whether you opted in to news.

News and updates (new paths, challenges and features) are off by default and are sent only if you turn them on under Profile → Reminders.

2.8 What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • We do not collect payment card details.

A journal can be very personal. We use what you write only to provide the app's features to you, never for advertising, and never send it to anyone except as described in 2.3.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the providers that make the app work:

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication and server functions. Processes your data on our instructions.
  • Anthropic — writes the AI chat replies, as described above.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging, and AdMob ads in the free version.
  • Apple — provides Sign in with Apple, push delivery and in-app purchases.

5. International transfers

Our providers operate infrastructure that may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism offered by the provider.

6. How long we keep it

Account, journal and practice data are kept while your account exists. AI conversations are not stored on our server. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for a limited period (90 days). Anonymous usage events are retained in aggregate; because they are not linked to your identity, they cannot be isolated and removed per person.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, write to info@kahatrainer.com from the address associated with your account. We answer within 30 days.

Deleting your account. In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. The account and all its synced data are erased immediately and permanently, the Sign in with Apple link is revoked, and the data on the device is cleared.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Practical controls — AI chat, tracking, ad consent, notifications — are listed on Your Privacy Choices.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules so that an account can only read its own records, the AI provider's key lives only on our server, never in the app, and chat conversations are saved on the device with iOS file protection.

9. Children

Stoic is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by your country's law, where it is higher). We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Not therapy

Stoic offers philosophy and reflection, not medical or psychological care. The AI chat is not a therapist and can be wrong. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, United States), call 188 (CVV, Brazil) or your local emergency number.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for changes that matter, tell you in the app before they take effect.
