Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This page lists every control you have over your data in Stoic, and exactly where to find it. For the full picture of what we collect and why, read the Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law (CCPA/CPRA) or comparable state laws. There is no opt-out to request, because there is no sale to opt out of.

The free version does show ads. Your choices over the advertising identifier those ads may use are described below.

Use Stoic without an account

An account is optional. In guest mode your journal and practice stay only on your iPhone and are never sent to us. An account is needed only to back up and sync your data and to use the AI chat.

AI chat

Messages go to the AI only when you use the chat, and only after you agree to the notice shown before the first conversation. A journal entry is sent only when you ask for a comment on it, and the weekly summary only when you tap to create it. If you don't use the chat, nothing is sent. Signing out deletes the conversations on the device and resets your agreement.

Turn off ad tracking

iOS asks whether Stoic may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not available to us or to our ad provider. You can change your answer at any time:

iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — find Stoic and switch it off. To clear the identifier for every app, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in the same screen.

Declining changes nothing about how the app works. Either paid plan removes ads altogether.

Ad consent (EEA, UK and other regions)

Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks how ads may use your data before any ad is shown. To review or change your choice: Stoic → Profile → Ad privacy.

Control notifications

Practice reminders are set and switched off under Profile → Reminders. On the same screen, News and updates is off unless you turn it on. To block all notifications: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Stoic.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account erases the account and all its synced data immediately, and clears the data on the device.

Access, export or correct your data

Write to info@kahatrainer.com from the email address on your account and tell us what you want:

  • A copy of your data — we send you an export of your account, journal and practice data.
  • A correction — we fix or update what is wrong.
  • Deletion — if you can't use the in-app option, we delete the account for you and confirm when it is done.

We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from the account email address before acting, so that nobody can reach someone else's data.

Usage statistics

Product analytics are recorded under a random identifier created on your device and are not connected to your account, and they never include what you write. Because they cannot be traced back to you, they cannot be isolated for individual deletion. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new identifier and breaks any continuity with the old one.

Questions

Anything unclear here is a defect in this page. Write to info@kahatrainer.com and we will answer, and fix the page.
