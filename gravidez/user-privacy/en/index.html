Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This page lists every control you have over your data in Nanita, and exactly where to find it. For the full picture of what we collect and why, read the Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law (CCPA/CPRA) or comparable state laws. Your pregnancy and health data are never used for ads and never sent to analytics. There is no opt-out to request, because there is no sale to opt out of.

The free version may show banner ads on content screens. Your choices over the advertising identifier those ads may use are described below.

Use the app without an account

An account is optional. In guest mode, your pregnancy data stays only on your phone and is never sent to our servers. You only need an account to sync between devices, invite your partner or pass the baby to Nanu.

Turn off ad tracking

iOS asks whether Nanita may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not available to us or to our ad provider, and ads are not personalized. You can change your answer at any time:

iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking — find Nanita and switch it off. To clear the identifier for every app, turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in the same screen.

Declining changes nothing about how the app works. The "No Ads" plan removes ads altogether.

Ad consent in Europe

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app also asks for consent through Google's form before showing personalized ads. To change your answer, turn off tracking as above, or delete and reinstall the app so the form is shown again. If you need help, write to info@kahatrainer.com.

Control notifications

Every kind of notification has its own switch under Profile → Notifications:

  • Morning update and Appointments — reminders scheduled on this phone.
  • Alerts — about your pregnancy, like a new week starting, sent by us.
  • News and tips — new features and content, sent by us. Off by default.

You can also turn off all notifications in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Nanita.

Manage who follows your pregnancy

Partners you invite see appointments, kicks, contractions, ultrasounds, bump and photos — never your symptoms, mood, weight, diary or exams. To see who is following or remove someone, go to Profile → Invite partner. If you are the partner, Profile → Stop following removes your access.

Delete your account

Profile → Delete account removes your account, the pregnancy you own with all its logs, lists and photos, and your partners' access, immediately and permanently. The account is shared with Nanu, so it is deleted there too, with the babies you own there.

In guest mode, Profile → Erase all data or deleting the app erases everything stored on the phone.

Access, export or correct your data

You can share a PDF summary of your pregnancy from the Progress screen at any time. For a full copy of what we store, write to info@kahatrainer.com from the email address on your account and tell us what you want:

  • A copy of your data — we send you an export of your account, pregnancy, logs and photos.
  • A correction — we fix or update what is wrong.
  • Deletion — if you can't use the in-app option, we delete the account for you and confirm when it is done.

We reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm from the account email address before acting, so that nobody can reach someone else's data.

Usage statistics

Product analytics never include health data and are recorded under a random identifier created on your device, not connected to your account. Because they cannot be traced back to you, they cannot be isolated for individual deletion. Deleting and reinstalling the app generates a new identifier and breaks any continuity with the old one.

Questions

Anything unclear here is a defect in this page. Write to info@kahatrainer.com and we will answer, and fix the page.
