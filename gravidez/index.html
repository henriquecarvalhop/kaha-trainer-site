Features

Everything about these 40 weeks.

One tap to log how you are today, and a full record ready for your next visit.

🤰

Today

Your week and day, due date and countdown, the baby's size this week, and symptoms, mood, weight, vitamins or water in one tap.

🩺

Prenatal care

Your next visit and the tests and vaccines due now, blood pressure and blood sugar, and a PDF summary for your doctor or midwife.

⏱️

Kicks and contractions

A kick counter with history, and a contraction timer with the 5-1-1 alert and a Live Activity on the Lock Screen.

📸

Journey and diary

The whole pregnancy, one line per week, with your notes and bump photos side by side.

📚

All 42 weeks

The baby, your body, what is common, a tip and a question for your visit — every week, plus short articles by topic.

🧳

Lists and names

Hospital bags, baby gear and your birth plan (with PDF), and baby names with your partner's votes.

Partner mode & Nanu

Together now. Ready for the baby.

Invite your partner with a code, and when the baby arrives, carry on in Nanu — our baby tracker — with the same account.

  • A 6-digit code. Your partner follows the week and gets tips of their own.
  • Only what you choose to share. Visits, kicks, contractions, ultrasounds, bump and photos. Your diary, symptoms and health logs stay private.
  • Live in labor. Your partner sees the contraction timer as it runs.
  • The baby was born. Save the date, weight and type of birth; Nanu opens with the baby already there.
  • If the pregnancy ends. A gentle option turns off weekly content and reminders at once.

FAQ

Questions worth asking first

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with a few banners on content screens. The optional No Ads plan removes them — monthly, yearly or once for life.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Nanita as a guest and everything stays on your iPhone. Create an account — with Apple, Google or your email — to back up your data, invite your partner and move to Nanu when the baby arrives.

What does my partner see?

The week, appointments, kicks, contractions, ultrasounds, bump and photos. Never your symptoms, mood, weight, diary or exams — and they cannot edit anything.

Is this medical advice?

No. Nanita offers general information and does not diagnose or treat anything. Always talk to your doctor or midwife, and call your local emergency number in an emergency.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish — the whole interface and the weekly content.
