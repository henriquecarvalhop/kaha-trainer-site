Support

We answer every email. Usually within two business days.

Write to info@kahatrainer.com
To get a useful answer faster, include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version (Profile → Settings, at the bottom), and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

I want to delete my account

Email info@kahatrainer.com from your account's address with the subject "Delete my account". We delete the account and its training data and write back to confirm.

I got a new phone — where is my data?

Sign in with the same account and everything comes back: programs, history, measurements, gyms and competitions. If you explored the app without an account, there was nothing to restore — that mode does not save.

The app is in the wrong language

Kaha Trainer follows your iPhone's language and can be overridden under Profile → Language. A language change only takes effect the next time the app is launched — close it fully and open it again.

My gym doesn't have the equipment the program asks for

Register your gym under Profile → Gyms and mark exactly what it has, then generate the program with that gym selected. You can keep several gyms and switch between them.

The app isn't suggesting heavier weights

A load suggestion appears only when every working set of an exercise was completed, each one hit its rep target, and you still had a rep in reserve on the sets before the last. Miss any of those and the app holds the weight on purpose.

An exercise is missing from the catalog

Tell us its name and, if you can, a link to a demonstration. The catalog grows from requests like that. You can also add your own exercise to a program in the meantime.

Calories show a dash instead of a number

The estimate needs your body weight. Record it under Progress, or enable Apple Health, and the number appears. The app shows a dash rather than guessing from a default weight.

How do I join a friend's competition?

Ask them for the competition code and enter it under the Competition tab. Your display name and results become visible to the other participants of that competition.

I found a bug

Please tell us. Steps to reproduce it are worth more than anything else you can send — what you tapped, what you saw, and what you expected instead.
