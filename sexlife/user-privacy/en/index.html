Your Privacy Choices

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Every control over your data in Lumbre and where to find it. The full picture is in the Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or share your data for advertising

The app has no ads and no tracking. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws). There is no sale to opt out of.

Use it without an account

An account is optional. Without it everything works, including connecting with your partner.

Statistics and errors

Profile → Privacy and data → Help improve the app. When off, nothing more is sent.

Export

Profile → Privacy and data → Export my data. The file is not encrypted and does not include photos: keep it somewhere safe.

Disconnect your partner

Profile → Connected partner → Disconnect. The iPhones stop updating each other and your packages are deleted from the server. What is already on your partner's iPhone stays there.

Erase everything and withdraw consent

Profile → Privacy and data → Erase everything. It erases the records on this iPhone and your packages on the server and disconnects your partner. Do this before deleting the app.

Delete the account

Profile → Account → Delete account. You choose whether to erase your records too. Deleting the account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Notifications

Profile → Notifications turns each alert and the discreet text on or off. To block them all: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Lumbre.

Location, camera and Face ID

iPhone Settings → Apps → Lumbre. The app works without these permissions. The lock is in Profile → Privacy.

Requests by email

Write to info@kahatrainer.com from your account email. We answer within 30 days. We cannot read, identify or erase encrypted records for you: use the buttons in the app.
