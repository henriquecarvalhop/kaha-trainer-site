Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data Lumbre ("the app") handles, why, and what you can do about it. The app is published by CloudArbitration LTDA ("we"), the data controller. Contact: info@kahatrainer.com.

1. In short

Lumbre is for adults 18+ and stores intimate data. What you record stays on your iPhone. If you connect your two iPhones, the data goes through our server end-to-end encrypted: we cannot open it. An account is optional and does not receive your records. There are no ads, no tracking, and we do not sell data. Usage statistics and error reports are sent only if you turn that option on, and never include what you record.

2. Intimate data and your consent

What you record can reveal your sex life, sexual orientation and health (for example, your menstrual cycle). The law treats this as sensitive data (LGPD, arts. 5 II and 11; GDPR, art. 9; consumer health data laws in some US states). We handle it only with your explicit consent, requested on the first screen. You can withdraw it at any time by erasing the data in Profile → Privacy and data → Erase everything.

Before recording something about another person, have their consent.

3. Data the app handles

3.1 What you record

  • Entries and calendar: date, duration, ratings, who started, place, protection, mood, positions, toys and notes.
  • A place on the map, only if you choose to mark it.
  • Positions, toys (with a photo, if you take one), missions, games, Desires answers, preferences, limits and menstrual cycle.

All of this stays on your iPhone. As with any app, it may be part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), which is yours and follows Apple's rules.

3.2 Connecting with your partner

Connecting is optional. The two iPhones exchange a code and create a key only they have. Data and photos are encrypted on the device (AES-256) before going through our server. We keep encrypted packages, their size, dates and a code that proves which device created them. We do not have the key. A copy of the key stays in each person's iCloud Keychain so you can change iPhones.

What your partner receives: both iPhones will hold the same entries, calendar, ratings, cycle, toys, missions, preferences and rules. Desires answers also go to your partner's iPhone; the screen shows only what you both agreed to. What reached your partner's iPhone stays there if you disconnect. Connect only with someone you trust.

3.3 Account (optional)

With email, Apple or Google. We keep your name and email and, with Apple, the code that lets us revoke the sign-in. Apple may give us a relay address. Passwords stay with the authentication provider and are not visible to us. Your records do not go to the account.

3.4 Couple plan

If you subscribe, the purchase is made through Apple. We receive the plan, dates, status and transaction identifiers, and a code that represents the couple without saying who you are, so you can both use it. We never see payment details.

3.5 Notifications

Reminders are created on the iPhone itself, with discreet text by default. If you allow notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging and keep: the device's delivery code, a random installation identifier, language, time zone, app version, the account (if any), your options and the code of your partner's package. The partner update alert never says what it is.

3.6 Statistics and errors (optional)

With the option on, we send neutral events, such as "opened the app", "viewed the home screen", "subscribed", with a random identifier, the version and the time. No event says what you do, with whom, when, or what you answered. Errors go through Firebase Crashlytics: device model, iOS and app version and the technical state at the time of the failure. We do not use Google Analytics.

3.7 Location, camera and photos

Location is requested only when you mark an entry on the map, and stays with that entry. The map is provided by Apple, which receives the area shown. The camera and photos are used only when you choose.

3.8 Face ID

The lock is handled by iOS. We have no access to your face or biometric data.

3.9 Technical data

As with any internet service, our providers receive the IP address and time of connections, used to run and protect the service.

5. Who we share with

We do not sell data. Only these take part:

  • Your partner, when you connect.
  • Supabase: database, sign-in and functions, in the United States. Your records reach it encrypted.
  • Google: Firebase Cloud Messaging and Crashlytics, and Google sign-in if you use it.
  • Apple: sign-in, purchases, notifications, map and iCloud Keychain.
  • Authorities, when the law requires. We have no way to hand over the content of encrypted records.

6. International transfers

Providers operate in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism.

7. How long we keep it

  • On the iPhone: until you erase the data or the app.
  • Encrypted packages: until you disconnect or erase everything. Invitations are deleted within 4 days. Packages that neither of you uses for 12 months are deleted; to stay connected after that you need to connect again, and what is on the iPhones stays there. Disconnect or use "Erase everything" before deleting the app: otherwise the packages remain encrypted on the server until then and we do not know whose they are.
  • Account: until you delete it. Purchase records may be kept without a link to the account for as long as the law requires.
  • Notification code: until you sign out, the code stops working or it is deleted.
  • Statistics: not linked to you. Error reports: Crashlytics retention (90 days).

8. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, erase, restrict, object and withdraw consent. In the app: export and erase in Profile → Privacy and data; delete the account in Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also write to info@kahatrainer.com; we answer within 30 days. Because records are encrypted and have no account, only you can export or erase them, in the app.

You may complain to a data protection authority: in Brazil, the ANPD; in Europe, the one in your country.

9. Security

End-to-end encryption between the iPhones, TLS connections, database access rules, Face ID lock (if you turn it on) and content hidden in the app switcher. No system is infallible: protect your iPhone with a passcode and connect only with someone you trust.

10. Adults only (18+)

The app is not for people under 18 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you learn of a case, write to us and we will erase what can be identified.

11. Not medical advice

The cycle, tips and predictions are only a record. They are not a contraceptive method and do not replace a health professional.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date. For important changes we tell you in the app and, when needed, ask for consent again.
