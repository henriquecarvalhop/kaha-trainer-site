Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one, including connecting with your partner. The account keeps your plan.

How do we connect our iPhones?

In Profile → Connect with your partner, one creates the code and the other types it. The code lasts 48 hours and only one person can join. Both need the latest version of the app.

What does my partner see?

Both iPhones will hold the same entries. Desires answers show only when you both agreed, and each person's mission is hidden from the other.

Can you read what we record?

No. What goes through the server is encrypted and only your two iPhones have the key.

I changed iPhones

With the same Apple ID and iCloud Keychain on, open Profile → Connect with your partner → Continue with…. Use it only on the iPhone that replaced the old one.

One of us subscribed and the other does not have the plan

Check that the iPhones are connected and open the app on both. On the buyer's iPhone, tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

How do I erase everything?

Profile → Privacy and data → Erase everything. Do this before deleting the app. For the account: Profile → Account → Delete account.

Is it a contraceptive method?

No. The cycle is only a record and does not replace a health professional.

I do not feel safe

Sex always needs consent. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number (911 in the US). US National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233.
