Features

The whole routine, without the notebook.

One tap to mark what's done today, and a full health record for the day you need it.

✅

Today, for every pet

Food, water, medicine, walks and flea treatment on one list, with reminders. Tap to mark done, skip what doesn't apply, see what's overdue.

💉

Health and vaccines

Vaccine timeline, weight, symptoms, hygiene and appointments. A PDF to take to the vet or hand to whoever looks after your pet.

🦮

Walks on a map

Start a walk and see the route, time and distance. GPS runs only while the walk does — never in the background otherwise.

📸

A diary of photos

Photos and notes of the moments worth keeping, for one pet or several at once.

📄

Documents at hand

Vaccination cards, exams and prescriptions in the pet's profile, with 200 MB of storage per account.

🐾

Every pet you have

Dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, rodents, reptiles and fish. No extra charge for the second pet — or the fifth.

Family & lost-pet QR

Care together. Get them home.

Invite the people who share the care, and give your pet a tag that lets whoever finds it reach you — showing only what you choose.

  • Family sharing. Invite with a link or code; everyone sees the same records, in real time.
  • Pet sitters. They log what they did, and their access ends on the date you set.
  • Who did it. Each record shows who fed, walked or gave the medicine.
  • Lost-pet QR. Print a tag or a poster. Scanning it opens your pet's page with the contacts you picked.
  • "I'm lost" alert. Mark your pet as lost and the page asks whoever finds it to call you.

FAQ

Questions worth asking first

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with ads — including several pets, family sharing, the vet PDF and the lost-pet QR code. The optional No ads purchase removes the ads, monthly, yearly or once for life.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Petlu as a guest and everything stays on your iPhone. Create an account — with Apple, Google or your email — to back up your data, share a pet with your family and turn on the lost-pet page.

Does it track my location?

Only during a walk you start, and only until you finish it. The route is saved with that walk so you can see it on a map.

What does someone see when they scan the QR code?

Your pet's name, photo, species and breed, your message, and only the contacts you chose to show — phone, WhatsApp or email. Never your address.

Is this veterinary advice?

No. Petlu helps you organize care and records; it does not replace your veterinarian. If your pet shows signs of an emergency, contact a veterinarian right away.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish — the whole interface. It follows your iPhone's language by default, and you can change it in the app.
