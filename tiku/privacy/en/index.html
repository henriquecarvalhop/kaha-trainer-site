Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what data Tiku ("the app") handles, why, and what you can do about it. The app is published by CloudArbitration LTDA ("we"), the data controller. Contact: info@kahatrainer.com.

1. In short

Without an account, everything you record stays on your device. With an account, your habits are also kept on our server so you can use them on another device. Competitions are shared with the people who join them. There are no ads, no tracking and no usage statistics, and we do not sell data.

2. Data the app handles

2.1 Without an account ("Just explore the app")

Habits, entries, plans, notes and settings stay on your device and in its backup (iCloud or computer), which is yours and follows Apple's rules. Nothing is sent to us.

2.2 Account (optional)

With email or Apple. We keep your name and email and, with Apple, the code that lets us revoke the sign-in when you delete the account. Apple may give us a relay address. Passwords stay with the authentication provider and are not visible to us.

2.3 Habits, synced to your account

With an account, your habits, daily entries, quantities, notes, plans and preferences are stored on our server and synced between your devices. Only you can read them.

2.4 Competitions

Competitions require an account. Whoever creates one chooses the name, rules, activities, dates and, optionally, a prize description. To join you need the competition's code. Everyone in the same competition sees: the name you use in competitions, your check-ins (activity, title, note, day and time, points, and optionally duration, distance, calories, steps, a place you type and a photo) and the ranking. People outside the competition, and people you blocked or who blocked you, do not see them.

Reports: when you report something, we keep the reason, your note and a copy of the reported content, so we can review it even if the author deletes it.

2.5 Camera and photos

Used only when you add a photo to a check-in. The photo is uploaded to our server and shown only to the competition's participants.

2.6 Notifications, widget and Siri

Reminders are created on the device itself; we do not send push notifications. The widget and Siri shortcuts read data from the device.

2.7 Data export

Profile → Backup → Export data creates a file on your device. Where it goes after that is up to you.

2.8 Technical data

As with any internet service, our providers receive the IP address and time of connections, used to run and protect the service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions, or to train artificial intelligence.

4. Who we share with

We do not sell data. Only these take part:

  • Other participants of the competitions you join, as described in section 2.4.
  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and functions, in the United States.
  • Apple: sign-in with Apple, notifications on the device and the App Store.
  • Authorities, when the law requires.

5. International transfers

Our providers operate in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or an equivalent mechanism.

6. How long we keep it

  • On the device: until you delete the data or the app.
  • Account, habits, competitions, check-ins and photos: until you delete them or delete the account. Deleting the account erases them from our server.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after they are closed, unless the law requires longer.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, erase, restrict, object and withdraw consent. In the app: export in Profile → Backup → Export data; delete the account in Profile → Account → Delete account; unblock people in Profile → Blocked people. You can also write to info@kahatrainer.com; we answer within 30 days.

You may complain to a data protection authority: in Brazil, the ANPD; in Europe, the one in your country.

8. Security

TLS connections, database access rules (each person reads only their own data and the competitions they belong to) and private photo storage. No system is infallible: protect your device with a passcode.

9. Children

Accounts and competitions are for people 13 and older (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you learn of a case, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date. For important changes we tell you in the app.
