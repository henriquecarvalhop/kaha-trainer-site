Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Just explore the app and everything stays on your device. The account takes your habits to another device and is required for competitions.

I created an account: what happened to what I already had?

It goes to the account together. Before that, the app saves a copy in the Files app.

How do I join a competition?

Ask whoever created it for the code and type it in Competitions → Join with a code. To create one, tap + in Competitions and share the code.

Someone posted something inappropriate

Press and hold the check-in to Report it or its author; to report the competition, use the competition's menu. You can also Block the person: you stop seeing each other in every competition. We review reports within 24 hours. Unblock in Profile → Blocked people.

Are the prizes guaranteed?

No. Prizes are arranged by whoever created the competition. Neither we nor Apple sponsor them.

Reminders do not appear

Check Profile → Notifications and, in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Tiku, that they are allowed.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Profile → Backup → Export data. To bring it back, Profile → Backup → Restore from a file.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account → Delete account. Your habits, competitions and photos are erased from our server.
